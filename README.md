# MediLog
MediLog is an iOS application designed to help users log, track, and manage their pharmacy visits, medication purchases, and health symptoms. With an intuitive, privacy-respecting interface, MediLog enables users to easily create detailed records of their medical visits, including photos of receipts or prescription labels, tagged symptoms, and medications purchased.

## Features

- **Add & View Pharmacy Visits**: Store visit details such as pharmacy name, date, symptoms, products bought, and associated tags.
- **Track Medications**: Keep an organized log of products purchased, including notes, expiry dates, and batch information.
- **Symptom Logging**: Document symptoms for each visit for better health tracking over time.
- **Photo Import**: Attach photos (e.g., prescriptions, receipts) to visits for reference.
- **Tag Management**: Easily categorize visits or medications with tags.
- **Delete/Edit Entries**: Remove or update existing entries securely.
- **Barcode Scanning**: Quick product lookup and entry with integrated barcode scanner.
- **Clean, Modern UI**: Built with SwiftUI for a seamless, native iOS experience.
- **Data Privacy**: All information is stored locally on your device using Core Data.

## Getting Started

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```
   git clone https://github.com/sulimanbadour1/MediLog.git
   ```

2. **Open the project** in Xcode (iOS 18+ recommended).

3. **Build & Run** on a simulator or your iOS device.

4. **Start Managing** your medication and health records easily and securely.
